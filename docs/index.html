Following the 12-politician reliable group beats SPY by +4.8% at 90 days. A high-conviction filter showed +7.9% — but an audit found structural issues; the live strategy runs the broader group.

Finding 4 — Counterintuitive

Exit 10 days after the sell filing, not before

Politicians sell 27 days before filing on average. Price actually rises +1.7% during this undisclosed window — don’t exit on the sell filing. But stop at +10 days after.

Caveats & validation

In-sample selection bias

The 12 “reliable” politicians were selected from the same 2022–2026 data the backtest runs on. Even with a strict excess >2% / ≥20 trades filter, selection inflates apparent returns. Sample sizes are small; high excess may reflect a specific period or sector.

Backtesting assumptions

Returns assume buying at the close on the filing date with zero slippage. STOCK Act data is self-reported with known errors. The 30–45 day disclosure lag means the actual trade is already history by the time you see it. Dataset covers a mostly bullish 2022–2026.

Walk-forward test

Done

Train on 2022–2023, test on 2024–2026. All-trades: 14% alpha retention (Sullivan/Suozzi left Congress; Mullin +0.8% OOS). HC: 66% retention. Key caveat: Moore, McCormick, and Fields all joined Congress Jan 2025 — a true OOS test for them needs ~mid-2026.

Remaining validations

Not run

Permutation test — shuffle politician→ticker assignments 1,000x; check if +4.8% is in the top 5%.
Transaction cost sensitivity — rerun at 0.25%, 0.5%, 1.0% round-trip friction.
Beta-adjusted alpha — if excess vanishes after beta adjustment, it’s high-vol exposure, not informational edge.
